### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment here gives me a sense of high discipline but is equally cohesive. The team members are extremely friendly and sociable. However, during working hours, the atmosphere becomes absolutely serious. Everyone fully focuses on their tasks without any private chatting or using personal phones. This professionalism and discipline create an ideal space, helping me maintain high concentration whenever I need to read technical documents, analyze logs, or configure systems. Nevertheless, it's a bit of a pity that the schedule to go to the office requires a pending approval process, so the actual number of days I got to work on-site with everyone was quite limited.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
The mentors and team admin are extremely enthusiastic and dedicated. What I appreciate most is their patience: even with technical issues I didn't fully grasp and had to ask about repeatedly, they still calmly and thoroughly explained every detail until I truly understood the problem. Throughout the implementation of the final internship project, everyone always created the most favorable conditions for me to successfully achieve my goals. Especially, there were times late at night when I messaged them asking for feedback on the project, and the mentors were still ready to respond and guide me enthusiastically. I really appreciate and am grateful for the time the mentors took to guide me throughout this internship.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
This is what I am most satisfied with during the internship. The assigned tasks closely align with my passion and career orientation, particularly in areas related to Security Operations Center (SOC), Linux OS security administration, and incident analysis. Being able to apply the knowledge from my school projects in a real-world environment helped me realize many things that theory could not fully demonstrate.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
This internship has been a major stepping stone for my mindset and professional skills. I didn't just practice logical analytical thinking and system trace investigation, but I also learned meticulousness and carefulness—vital elements when working in this field. Specifically, the internship helped me gain a deeper and more practical understanding of the management and deployment processes in Cloud environments. Besides improving my tool-usage and technical reporting skills, the projects and knowledge accumulated over the past time have become extremely valuable material, greatly contributing to perfecting my CV-writing skills and highlighting my core competencies to prepare for my upcoming career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The teamwork spirit in the team is very egalitarian and mutually respectful. Even though I am just an intern, I was always listened to when providing analytical perspectives on a risk or a certain abnormal sign. When there was an urgent task or a complex issue needed to be "dissected," everyone was always ready to sit down together. The feeling of directly contributing to the overall security process of the system makes me feel very proud.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The company creates maximum optimal conditions for students. The flexible timing allowed me to successfully complete my final projects and school reports while ensuring the progress of my tasks at the company. The allowance level is also very reasonable, reflecting the company's recognition of the interns' efforts.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?  

Being able to work in a truly serious, distraction-free environment and directly "touching" real-world problems of a professional defense system. Above all is the dedicated accompaniment and guidance, day or night, from the mentors.

- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?

The company could consider optimizing the office schedule approval process to be faster, allowing interns to have more on-site working days. Working directly at the office brings much higher interaction and learning efficiency.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?  

Definitely yes. Especially for those who have a deep passion for the Cloud and prefer a highly disciplined, focused working environment. This is an ideal hands-on environment to gain practical experience and systematically hone technical thinking, thanks to the extremely dedicated guidance of the mentor team.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations
- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?  

I hope the office registration schedule process can be approved faster or that there could be more on-site office days to increase bonding time and work directly with the team.
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?  

I strongly desire the opportunity to become an official member of FCAJ Silver Star or to continue accompanying the program in deeper and more challenging security projects.
